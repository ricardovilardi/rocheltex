Our pillars

One standard for quality, environment, people and governance

Our management rests on four pillars, aligned with internationally recognised ISO standards and ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) criteria.

01

Quality and excellence in automotive

ISO 9001 · IATF 16949
  • Customer focus and a zero-defects philosophy
  • Continuous improvement and risk management through FMEA
  • Strict compliance with product safety requirements
02

Environmental commitment and decarbonisation

ISO 14001
  • Protecting the environment and efficient use of natural resources
  • Circular economy: waste reduction, reuse and recycling
  • Active reduction of our carbon footprint
03

People, safety and well-being

ISO 45001
  • Safe and healthy work environments
  • Preventive culture and the right to stop work in the face of any risk (Stop Work)
  • Active staff involvement in continuous improvement
  • Equal opportunities and absolute rejection of forced or child labour
04

Governance and business ethics

Ethics · Compliance · Supply chain
  • Zero tolerance of bribery and corruption
  • Rigorous compliance with legislation and customer requirements
  • Requiring the same standards from our supply chain
Code of ethics

A culture built on integrity

Our Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct is mandatory for all Rocheltex staff, and extends the same principles to the suppliers, customers and institutions we work with.

  • Respect, diversity and zero tolerance of any form of discrimination or harassment
  • Occupational health and safety as a non-negotiable principle
  • Confidentiality and data protection, in line with the GDPR
  • Zero tolerance of bribery in any commercial relationship
  • Responsible use of company assets and transparency towards third parties
Human rights

The same standard at every workplace

Our Human and Labour Rights Policy sets the mandatory guidelines governing relations with managers, employees and partners at all our sites and operations.

  • Equal treatment and non-discrimination, with specific protection for the LGBTI community
  • Absolute rejection of forced labour and special vigilance against child labour in the supply chain
  • Fair working hours, guaranteed breaks and paid holidays
  • Respect for freedom of association and collective bargaining

Certifications

Audited processes in every pillar

ISO 9001 backs our quality; ISO 14001, our environmental management; ISO 45001, the health and safety of our team. IATF 16949, PSB and PSCR complete the quality framework for the automotive sector.

Reporting channel

A confidential channel, with no retaliation

We provide employees and partners with an Ethics Channel to report any irregularity in good faith. We guarantee confidentiality, no retaliation and an objective investigation, in compliance with Law 2/2023.
