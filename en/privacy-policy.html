2. What data we process and where it comes from

4. Retention period

We will keep your data for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and, where applicable, for the duration of the business relationship. After that, they will be duly blocked for the limitation periods of any legal liabilities that may arise, and deleted once those periods have elapsed.

5. Recipients and international transfers

We will not disclose your data to third parties, except where legally required (for example, to Public Administrations, judges and courts). Technology service providers (web hosting, email and maintenance) acting as data processors under a contract pursuant to Article 28 GDPR may have access to it.

As a general rule, we do not transfer data outside the European Economic Area. If a provider were located outside it, the transfer would be covered by a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses. If you agree to load the Google map on the Contact page, Google LLC may process data about your browsing in accordance with its privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of the prior processing), by writing to jss@rocheltex.es stating the right you wish to exercise. We may ask you for a copy of a document proving your identity.

We will reply within one month at most. If you consider that your rights have not been properly dealt with, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration, in line with the level of risk.

8. Minors

This Website is not aimed at children under 14. If you become aware that a minor has given us data without the authorisation of their legal representatives, please write to us so we can delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy policy to adapt it to legal or operational changes. The version in force is the one published on this page, with its update date.
